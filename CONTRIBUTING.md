# Contributions

Contribute independently written descriptions of protocol behavior and clearly distinguish static inference from hardware observations. Use synthetic identifiers in examples and redact credentials and personal device identifiers from captures.

Submit only material you are entitled to contribute under the repository’s documentation license. Attribution alone does not authorize copying third-party code, manual text, images, or other assets. Do not add vendor firmware, app packages, updater binaries, extracted code, secrets, or flashing workflows to this protocol publication.

Retain acquisition sources, dates, versions, hashes, applicable terms, and analysis records with the private research materials. Public availability alone does not establish permission to redistribute or analyze software. Do not claim vendor approval, compatibility, or legal clearance without evidence.
